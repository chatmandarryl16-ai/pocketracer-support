Privacy Policy

This policy describes the iPhone app PocketRacer: Neon Sprint ("PocketRacer"). The game can be played without an account. Racing does not require an internet connection.

Game progress on your device

Best time, coin total, selected car, sound, and haptics preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. PocketRacer has no game account or game backend. Removing the app and its data removes this saved progress.

Advertising

PocketRacer uses Google AdMob to display banner ads on the menu and race-results screens when ad requests are permitted. Ads are not shown during a race. Google and its partners may process data such as device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and analytics. Some of this data may be linked to your device or used to track activity across apps or websites if you permit tracking. The exact handling depends on your location, consent choices, device settings, and the ads served. Read Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Your choices

Where required, PocketRacer asks for advertising consent before requesting ads and offers Privacy Options in the game settings. You can also manage app tracking permission in iPhone Settings. Declining tracking does not prevent you from racing. You may see ads that are less relevant to you.

Children

PocketRacer is a general-audience arcade game. It does not ask players to create accounts or provide personal details. If you believe a child has shared personal information through an ad or support request, please contact us using the support page below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its advertising changes. The date at the top will show the latest revision. For privacy questions or support, visit the PocketRacer support page.
