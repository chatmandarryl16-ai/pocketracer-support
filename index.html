PocketRacer is a four-lane iPhone sprint racer. Here are the essentials for getting back in the race.

01

Drive

Hold SPEED to accelerate. Move the joystick left or right to steer. Hold BRAKE to slow down or reverse.

02

Win

Race three other cars down a single straight track. Collect coins, cross the finish line, and try to beat your best time.

03

Pause

Tap the pause button during a race. The game also pauses when you leave the app. Resume, restart, or return to the menu.
